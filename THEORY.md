# Theory and Mathematics

## Queueing Formulation ($M/M/c$)
A single security gate with $c$ parallel lanes is modeled as an $M/M/c$ queue where arrivals follow a Poisson process at rate $\lambda$ (spectators/min) and each lane serves spectators at rate $\mu$ (spectators/min/lane):

$$\rho = \frac{\lambda}{c\mu}, \qquad \rho < 1$$

## Inverse-Transform Sampling and Erlang-C
Exponential inter-arrival and service durations are generated from $U \sim \text{Uniform}(0, 1)$ via:

$$X = -\frac{\ln(1 - U)}{\text{rate}}$$

For offered load $a = \lambda/\mu$ and $\rho < 1$, steady-state Erlang-C probability of waiting ($P_w$) and mean queue wait ($W_q$) are:

$$P_w = \frac{\frac{a^c}{c!(1-\rho)}}{\sum_{k=0}^{c-1}\frac{a^k}{k!} + \frac{a^c}{c!(1-\rho)}}, \qquad W_q = \frac{P_w}{c\mu - \lambda}$$

## Two-Stage Surge & Monte Carlo Convergence
For a two-stage checkpoint (ticket check feeding security screening) under a time-varying arrival surge $\lambda(t)$, arrivals are generated by thinning with dominating rate $\lambda_{\max} \ge \lambda(t)$. Across $N$ independent seed replications, sample mean error scales proportionally to $N^{-1/2}$, supporting 95% $t$-confidence intervals.
